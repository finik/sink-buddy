SINK BUDDY - print package
==========================

Material: PETG for the body, divider, legs, feet and spout (they sit wet, get dish soap and hot water).
The eyes and smile can be PLA in any color: they're small, decorative, and glued in.
No supports needed on any part. All parts are already oriented for printing.

0. BASE TEST (optional: the arches are where the last test had them; only the back got shorter)
   smiley_base_test.stl   175 x 135 x 2 mm. 110 mm between the arches. Front edge on the sink edge, arrow points at the sink.

1. BODY   smiley_body.stl   175 x 141 x 133 mm, PETG (black)
   Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% gyroid infill.

2. T INSERT   smiley_divider.stl   88 x 6 x 104 mm, PETG
   The tower has a fixed middle wall (left and right halves). The T drops into EITHER half at mid-depth
   and splits that half into a front and a back pocket (about 84 x 40 mm each).
   It slides down between rails on the middle wall and the side wall; its two ears rest on those rims.
   Turn it around to use it in the other half. Lift it out to clean. Water runs underneath it.
   Prints upside down, standing on its top edge and ears. Add a brim (5 mm) so the tall thin plate stays put.

3. FACE   smiley_eyes.stl (2 eyes) and smiley_smile.stl, PLA, any contrasting color
   Eyes print dome up, smile prints face down (1.9 mm flat strip).
   The body has a 1 mm deep pocket for each eye and a 2 mm groove for the smile, 0.15 mm clearance per side.
   Glue: a thin film of CA (super glue) or 2-part epoxy in the pocket/groove, press in.
   Epoxy holds better on PETG; scuff the pocket with sandpaper first if using CA.

4. SMALL PARTS (one plate, PETG)
   smiley_leg_left.stl, smiley_leg_right.stl   lying on their flat inner side
   smiley_foot_left.stl, smiley_foot_right.stl one of each (mirror images), flat sole down
   smiley_spout.stl                            standing upright
   Use 4 walls / 100% infill for the legs and spout: their pegs carry the load.

ASSEMBLY
   Legs:  push the D-shaped hip peg into its socket in the front of the base until the bumps click in
          (flat side of the peg toward the center). 0.15 mm clearance per side; a drop of CA glue if loose.
   Feet:  left foot on the left shin, right on the right (the D-socket only fits one way); flat heel toward the counter.
   Spout: press the tube into the angled hole between the legs. Glue if loose.

HOW IT DRAINS
   The back section's floor slopes to the front center -> tunnel (20 x 8 mm) -> sponge bowl -> V trough ->
   spout -> sink. Ribs keep the bottle and brushes level above the water. The T insert stops above the
   ribs, so water flows under it. Pointed windows along the foot of the middle wall let water cross
   between the halves and leave no tight corner to scrub.

KEY SIZES
   Back section: 4" deep inside (rib top to rim), split by the divider into two halves of about 78 x 82 mm,
   narrower at the front where the arches come in. EKOLN (58 x 80) fits either half with ~0.5-1 mm to spare.
   Sponge bowl: 83 mm wide sponge standing up, 30 mm thick assumed.
   Counter: sink edge to wall 6"; fixtures 2" diameter, centers 4" from the wall and 6" apart.
